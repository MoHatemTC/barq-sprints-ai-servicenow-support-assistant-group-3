# Retrieval Threshold Analysis & Recommendation

**Recommended Similarity Threshold:** `0.710`

### 1. Evaluation Metrics Addressed
This evaluation harness enforces two critical metrics outlined in the BARQ Systems RAG Research Guide:
* **Hit Rate:** Ensuring that at least one valid, relevant KB article chunk appears in the top-K retrieved results.
* **Abstention (Refusal Correctness):** Ensuring that the system correctly says "I don't know" to unanswerable, out-of-scope queries rather than hallucinating a response.

### 2. Observed Score Distribution & Separation
To determine the optimal confidence threshold, we evaluated 12 answerable incidents against 3 unanswerable negative controls using live vectors generated by `gemini-embedding-2` and retrieved from the Qdrant cluster. 

* **Answerable Incidents (True Positives):** For queries where the correct article was successfully retrieved, the lowest valid similarity score observed was **0.795**.
* **Negative Controls (True Negatives):** Unanswerable, out-of-scope queries produced distinctly lower confidence scores, maxing out at **0.624**.

### 3. Trade-off Justification
By calculating the mathematical midpoint between the highest negative score (0.624) and the lowest valid score (0.795), we establish a recommended production threshold of **0.710**.

* Choosing a lower threshold (e.g., `0.60`) would allow the highest-scoring negative controls to bypass the abstention gate, triggering false-positive AI drafts for out-of-scope IT requests.
* Choosing a higher threshold (e.g., `0.85`) would artificially degrade our Hit Rate, causing the AI to frequently abstain on valid IT incidents.
* At **0.710**, we establish a strict boundary that perfectly separates the observed valid hits from the noise. While the current baseline Hit Rate stands at 25.0%, maintaining this strict threshold ensures we do not artificially inflate our accuracy metrics by allowing hallucinations. Future sprints will focus on tuning the chunking parameters and embedding strategies to pull more valid incidents above this 0.710 boundary.
